# Community Guidelines

Atlas is a small project. These are guidelines, not a legal instrument.

## The standard

Be straight with people and assume they are doing the same. That is most of it.

Concretely:

- **Critique work, not people.** "This will deadlock when two workers claim the
  same lease" is useful. "This is amateur code" is not.
- **Say what you actually mean.** Indirect hostility is still hostility.
- **Assume competence.** Someone asking a basic question is new, not stupid.
  Everyone was new.
- **Accept a no.** Atlas has deliberate scope limits. If a request is declined
  as out of scope, that is a decision, not an opening position.
- **Do not demand free labour.** This is maintained by one person. Nobody here
  owes you a fix on your timeline.

## Not welcome

Harassment, sexual attention or advances, slurs and demeaning remarks about
who someone is, sustained disruption, doxxing, and using the project's spaces
to promote unrelated products.

## Scope

These guidelines apply in the repository, discussions, issues, pull requests
and any official Atlas chat space, and to conduct elsewhere that is directed
at people in this community.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/hellnight333/atlas/security/advisories/new)
(private) or a direct message. Include links.

Responses, depending on severity: a private word, a public correction, removal
of the offending content, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. There is no
committee and no appeals board — one maintainer, using judgement, in good
faith. Vexatious reports are themselves a problem.

## A note on the alpha

Rough edges are expected right now, and reporting them bluntly is fine and
useful. "This install flow is confusing and here is where I got lost" is a
gift. There is a difference between blunt and contemptuous, and everyone here
knows where it is.
