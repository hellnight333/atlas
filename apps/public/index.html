Websites for small businesses, built and kept working.

Qevik researches a business, builds it a site, tests it, publishes it and checks that what a customer receives is what was intended.

What it does

Researches first

Reads the existing site and records what is there, what is missing, and what could not be verified — with the evidence for each.

Builds and verifies

Generates the site, runs its tests, publishes it, then opens the published page in a browser to confirm it works.

Asks before publishing

Anything reaching the public waits for a person to approve the exact change, with the target and content shown in full.
