About

Catering is the part you taste. The rest is planning.

AHS is a Dubai event caterer working across two arms — corporate and private — with food, décor and staffing handled by one team rather than three suppliers.

Every event is quoted bespoke. There is no package to pick from, because a gala for four hundred and a wedding for eighty are not the same job. What stays constant is the kitchen behind it: halal-certified, HACCP-certified, and used to working to someone else's timings.
