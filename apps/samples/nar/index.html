The fire is the whole kitchen.

There is no gas line. Everything that reaches a table has been cooked over burning wood — the vegetables in the embers, the fish on the grate, the bread against the wall of the oven.

It makes the kitchen slower and the cooking less forgiving, which is the point. A cook who cannot turn a dial has to pay attention.
