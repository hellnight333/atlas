Before you call

Is there a call-out charge?

Only if you decide not to go ahead after the technician has assessed the job. If the work is done, the visit is included in the price.

How quickly can someone come?

Most same-day requests placed before 4pm are attended the same evening. AC in summer is the exception — those slots fill early.

Are parts included in the estimate?

No. The range above covers labour and the visit. If a part is needed, the technician quotes it before fitting anything.

Do you work on Fridays?

Yes, from 2pm. Emergencies are covered every day from 7am to 10pm.
